# Product Context

## Vision
FieldFlux helps field service businesses grow through intelligent marketing automation and actionable insights.

## Goals
- Unify content, leads, reviews, and analytics in one workflow.
- Automate routine marketing with safe AI assistance.
- Support multi-tenant, white-label deployments.

## Non-Goals
- Full-blown CRM or dispatch system.
- Deep ad-buy optimization (beyond basic guidance).

## Personas
- Owner/Operator: needs simple automation and clear ROI.
- Marketing Manager: needs scheduling, analytics, and collaboration.

## Outcomes
- More qualified leads, faster content delivery, and transparent performance reporting.
